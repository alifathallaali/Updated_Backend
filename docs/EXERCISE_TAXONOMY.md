# Exercise Taxonomy

The taxonomy groups executable and planned exercises into pharma functional packs.
Planned names are discovery/roadmap metadata only; they are not added to the executable manifest until analytics and validation exist.

Packs: Market & Commercial, Forecasting & Planning, Sales & SFE, Commercial Finance,
Market Access & HEOR, Medical Affairs, Supply Chain & Procurement, Tender,
Quality, Retail & Category Management, Launch & GTM.

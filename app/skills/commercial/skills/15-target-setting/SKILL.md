# Target Setting Skill

## Objective
Set defensible annual sales or volume targets using top-down, bottom-up or hybrid planning.

## Framework
Historical baseline → market growth → product potential → territory/HCP potential → capacity → strategic priorities → target scenarios.

## Critical rule
A general territory percentage is only a reference for total market potential unless product-specific evidence supports applying it to a product target.

## Outputs
Top-down target, bottom-up target, hybrid target, assumptions, gap analysis, scenarios and SMART targets.

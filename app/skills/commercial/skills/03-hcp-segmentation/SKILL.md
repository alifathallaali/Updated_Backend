# HCP Segmentation Skill

## Objective
Segment healthcare professionals based on commercially relevant and ethically appropriate variables.

## Inputs
- Specialty
- Geography
- Prescription behavior when legitimately available
- Potential
- Engagement
- Access
- Scientific interest
- Channel preference

## Framework
1. Define objective.
2. Establish permissible data.
3. Segment by potential and behavior.
4. Profile segments.
5. Define engagement strategy.
6. Recommend channel/frequency.

## Outputs
HCP segments, personas, priority tiers, engagement strategy.

## Compliance
Use only legitimate, appropriately sourced data. Do not infer sensitive personal attributes.

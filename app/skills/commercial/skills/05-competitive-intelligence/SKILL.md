# Competitive Intelligence Skill

## Objective
Turn competitor information into strategic implications.

## Inputs
- Competitor brands
- Market share
- Growth
- Pricing where legitimate
- Product attributes
- Public promotional messaging
- Clinical/public evidence
- Access information

## Framework
1. Define competitive set.
2. Compare products.
3. Identify competitor strengths.
4. Identify weaknesses only when supported.
5. Detect strategic moves.
6. Assess likely threats.
7. Recommend response options.

## Output
Competitor map, threat matrix, response recommendations, evidence log.

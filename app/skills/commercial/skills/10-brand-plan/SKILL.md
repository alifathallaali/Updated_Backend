# Brand Plan Skill

## Objective
Create an integrated annual pharmaceutical brand plan.

## Required sections
1. Executive summary
2. Market situation
3. Customer situation
4. Competitive situation
5. Brand performance
6. Diagnosis
7. Objectives
8. Strategy
9. Key initiatives
10. Field force
11. Medical
12. Market access
13. Omnichannel
14. Budget
15. KPIs
16. Risks
17. Assumptions
18. 90-day priorities

## Planning modes
- Top-down
- Bottom-up
- Hybrid/iterative

## Rule
Targets must be traceable to assumptions, historical performance, market potential and strategic choices.

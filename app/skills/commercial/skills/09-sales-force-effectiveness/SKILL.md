# Sales Force Effectiveness Skill

## Objective
Optimize field force deployment, targeting, coverage, frequency and productivity.

## Inputs
- Territory potential
- Sales
- Rx where available
- HCP universe
- Rep capacity
- Call activity
- Geography
- Product priorities

## Framework
1. Define universe.
2. Estimate potential using valid evidence.
3. Segment territories/HCPs.
4. Allocate coverage.
5. Set frequency.
6. Check capacity.
7. Optimize deployment.
8. Define KPIs.

## Important
A general territory percentage must not automatically become a product-level target. Product targets require product-specific evidence.

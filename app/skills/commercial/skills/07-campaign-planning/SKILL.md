# Campaign Planning Skill

## Objective
Design measurable pharmaceutical marketing campaigns.

## Framework
1. Business objective
2. Audience
3. Insight
4. Behavior/change objective
5. Core message
6. Message hierarchy
7. Channel mix
8. Content
9. Activation
10. Measurement

## Output
Campaign brief, channel plan, content requirements, KPI framework.

## QA
Every tactic must link to an objective and measurable KPI.

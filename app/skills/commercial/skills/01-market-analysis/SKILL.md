# Market Analysis Skill

## Objective
Analyze a pharmaceutical market, segment, molecule, brand, territory, or channel and identify commercial opportunities.

## Inputs
- Period
- Market definition
- Sales value/units
- Brand
- Molecule
- Company
- Therapeutic class
- Geography/territory
- Optional Rx/TRx/NRx, patient, epidemiology or access data

## Framework
1. Define market universe.
2. Validate data granularity.
3. Calculate size and growth.
4. Analyze market share.
5. Analyze contribution and concentration.
6. Identify growth leaders and declining segments.
7. Detect anomalies.
8. Identify opportunity areas.

## Outputs
- Market snapshot
- Growth analysis
- Share analysis
- Segment opportunity map
- Key insights
- Recommended actions
- Data gaps

## QA
- Check period consistency.
- Check units vs value.
- Check duplicate records.
- Do not infer Rx potential from territory percentages without evidence.

# Launch Strategy Skill

## Objective
Plan pharmaceutical product launches across commercial, medical, access and field dimensions.

## Framework
Market → Customer → Competition → Access → Positioning → Readiness → Launch → Scale

## Workstreams
- Market opportunity
- Segmentation
- Positioning
- KOL/medical engagement
- Market access
- Supply/readiness
- Field force
- Omnichannel
- Budget
- KPIs

## Output
Launch roadmap, critical milestones, risks, dependencies and KPIs.
